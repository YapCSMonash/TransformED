MISSION BRIEFING

How to Play & Escape

  • Educational Mission: Apply Year 3 pharmacy knowledge on the basic pharmacologic management, safety screening, interactions, and counseling for Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) and Erectile Dysfunction (ED).
  • Three-Room Structure: Room 1 and Room 2 open right away and can be completed in either order. Room 3 is locked until you collect both document fragments and enter the four-digit code.
  • Timer, Life Points & Penalties: You have a 20-minute countdown timer and 100 starting life points. Every incorrect answer deducts 10 life points. Reaching 0 life points or running out of time triggers Game Over. Optional hints are available in each room.
  • Document Fragments & Vault Code: Completing Room 1 reveals Fragment 1; completing Room 2 reveals Fragment 2. Once both are collected, they join together to reveal the four-digit code required to unlock Room 3.

Learning Objective: Differentiate pharmacologic mechanisms, onset of action, and adverse effect profiles in the management of Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH).

Question 1.2 (Multiple Selection)

A 68-year-old man with moderate-to-severe lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS) and a prostate volume of 45 mL is initiated on combination therapy with dutasteride and doxazosin. Which of the following counseling points are clinically appropriate? (Select all that apply.)

Consider first-dose blood pressure drops with doxazosin, pregnancy precautions for 5α-reductase inhibitors, and how long dutasteride takes to affect PSA and prostate size.
